# Decisions and assumptions

| # | Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem statement | `docs/Ecommerce_Analytics_Usecase.docx` is not in the repo; the pasted brief is the source of truth. | File missing. |
| 2 | Suppliers file | `suppliers_json.txt` renamed to `suppliers.json` at the owner's request (contents unchanged). | It is a JSON array. |
| 3 | Ambiguous slash dates | Parse per value: first part > 12 means DD/MM; second part > 12 means MM/DD; both <= 12 defaults to **MM/DD**. Such rows get `*_date_ambiguous = 1` (orders 63, payments 56, returns 9). | Both layouts occur in the same column. Among unambiguous orders, MM/DD (52) outnumbers DD/MM (43). Unconfirmed by the data owner. |
| 4 | Duplicate winner | Most complete cleansed record, then smallest content hash, then lowest source row. Losers go to quarantine (`DUPLICATE_EXACT` or `DUPLICATE_CONFLICT`). | No `last_updated` column. The hash step makes the result independent of file order. |
| 5 | Product list price <= 0 | Keep the product, set `unit_price` to NULL and `price_issue`. Not quarantined. | Revenue uses the line's own price; quarantining would orphan order lines. It also lets a complete duplicate win (P003, P060). |
| 6 | Order-line rules | Quarantine: quantity <= 0, negative or missing unit price, discount outside 0-100. | Revenue cannot be computed reliably. |
| 7 | `line_total` | Recomputed with Decimal `ROUND_HALF_UP`; source kept in `source_line_total`; `line_total_mismatch` if differing by > 0.01 (29 silver rows). | Source totals are often double or half the expected value. |
| 8 | Orphan foreign keys | Kept and flagged (`is_orphan_*`); in gold they point at the `-1` Unknown member. | Brief: do not drop. |
| 9 | Near-duplicate customers | Same normalised email **and** phone under different ids are flagged in `dup_group_id`, not merged. | Orders reference both ids; merging is irreversible. |
| 10 | Supplier rating scale (see #21 for KPI 6) | Detected from the file: smallest of 5/10/100 that contains the max rating. Found 10 (max 9.4). `rating_norm = rating / scale` (0-1). | Document-and-detect rather than assume. |
| 11 | Returned quantity | Refund >= line total: full line quantity (`FULL`). Otherwise `quantity * refund / line_total` (`PROPORTIONAL`). Missing refund or orphan item: NULL (`UNKNOWN`). `refund_exceeds_line` flags refunds above line value (4). | `returns.csv` has no quantity. |
| 12 | Return before order | 29 returns precede their order (26 unambiguous dates). Flagged `return_before_order`, kept. | A source defect; dropping would lose refunds. |
| 13 | Date outliers | 6 orders dated 2020-2022 and 2 signups in 2027 are kept as stated. | Dates are valid; cause unknown. |
| 14 | Reference date | Latest order date in silver, never today. Stored in `run_params`. | Brief. |
| 15 | `_loaded_at` | Real load time, so it differs per run; idempotency comparisons exclude it. | Lineage needs a true timestamp. |
| 16 | Commits | No commits are made by the assistant. | Owner preference. |
| 17 | KPI revenue scope | Net value = recomputed `line_total` minus **Approved** refunds, each capped at its line, attributed to the order month. KPI 1, 4, 5, 6 and margin risk use Completed + Returned orders (the brief excludes only Cancelled/Pending); KPI 2, 3 and 7 use `Completed` only, as worded. Returned orders keep their revenue and lose their approved refunds once, line by line. | No double subtraction of refunds; Pending/Rejected refunds are not money returned. Alternatives are written in each KPI's SQL header. |
| 18 | Supplier score | Composite = 0.40 revenue (scaled to the top supplier) + 0.30 (1 - return rate scaled to the worst supplier) + 0.30 rating on its own scale; a missing component is left out and the weights rescaled. | Each component is 0-1, so each weight means what it says (a revenue *share* of ~0.1 would make the revenue weight almost irrelevant). |
| 19 | Payment reconciliation | Aggregate payments, lines and quarantined payment rows per order before joining; paid = Success rows only; status by `payment_tolerance`; every order reported, plus orphan payments; duplicates removed in silver are counted back from `quarantine_payments` and flagged. | Avoids double counting; duplicates are flagged, not lost. |
| 20 | Missing temporal fields | Retain rows, expose warning flags, and write them to `quality_issues`; do not silently convert them into valid dates. | Analysts can choose whether to exclude them for a specific KPI. |
| 21 | Supplier rating scale in KPI 6 | Supersedes #10 for the scorecard: ratings are on mixed scales (15 suppliers rated 1.6-5.0, 3 rated 7.5-9.4), so each rating is normalised on its own scale: > 5 means out of 10, otherwise out of 5. `dim_supplier.rating_norm` (all / 10) is not used by the KPI. | The brief says ratings were entered on an inconsistent scale; dividing all by 10 halves every 5-point rating. Interim assumption: a 10-point rating of 5 or below cannot be told apart and is read as out of 5. |
| 22 | Orphaned / ambiguous customers in KPI 2 | Kept out of the top-10 ranking and reported separately with count and value (`kpi_02b`, shown under KPI 2). | A customer is never ranked on a guessed identity; merging near-duplicates is irreversible. |
| 24 | Blank product category | In silver, after de-duplication, an `Unknown` category is filled from `sub_category` when every other product with that sub-category has the same category (5 products: P008 Fashion, P017 and P059 Home & Kitchen, P054 Sports, P066 Grocery). Flagged `category_inferred` and logged in `quality_issues` (`CATEGORY_INFERRED`). Sub-categories that map to more than one category are left Unknown. | In this data each sub-category belongs to exactly one category, so the fill is unambiguous. It moved ~₹1.9M of KPI 1 revenue out of `Unknown`; only lines for product ids missing from the product file stay Unknown. |
| 23 | One KPI implementation | KPIs are SQL only (`kpis/bigquery/*.sql`, run on BigQuery; SQLite twins in `kpis/` for offline runs and tests). The earlier Python implementation (`medallion/kpis.py`) was removed. | The brief asks for SQL and window functions; one implementation avoids two sets of numbers. |
